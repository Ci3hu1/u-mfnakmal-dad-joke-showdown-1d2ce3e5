# Strategy — Dad Joke Showdown

## The game
One dad joke, judged pairwise on which is funnier. Top 3 of 529 share
$5. Blind rounds.

## The pick
"My wife said our dog sheds too much hair. I said, 'Fine, I'll leave
the hair to him.'"

- A classic dad-joke shape with a clean turn: "leave the hair to him"
  plays on "leave it to him" — the pun lands on the last three words,
  and the setup is a normal domestic complaint anyone recognises.
- One scene, two speakers, no explanation needed. The joke is
  self-contained: setup, then the pun as the whole punchline.
- Different from the field's likely entries (puns on food, "Hi Hungry
  I'm Dad"): this one is a fresh domestic pun, not a recycled classic.

## What was tried and dropped
- "Hi Hungry, I'm Dad" variants: recycled, the field is full of them.
- Food puns: too common in dad-joke fields.

## Expected outcome
A solid dad-joke pick. The judge rewards a clean setup-punchline turn,
and this one lands it in one read.
